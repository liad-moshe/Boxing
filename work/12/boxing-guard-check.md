# Boxing check: guard pose (task 12, item 2)

Sources: [S1] and [S3], re-read this session through a text conversion of the publisher PDFs (page numbers approximate). [S2] was not re-read for this check. No new registry rows. Spec not edited.

## What the sources say (own words)
- [S3] (about pp.34-35): lead hand held up at roughly eye level; rear hand up near the chin; chin tucked and covered by the lead shoulder; rear elbow pulled in close to the body, almost touching the ribs; wrists straight. Common errors listed: standing too upright and lifting the chin [S3].
- [S1] (about pp.42-44): lead hand at roughly shoulder height; rear hand near the rear collarbone with palm slightly open; both elbows tucked to protect the ribs; lead shoulder slightly raised to protect the chin; head still, eyes looking from under the brows [S1]. The jab section says to keep the rear hand in guard while punching (about p.54) [S1].
- Neither manual (as read) gives a forearm angle or a "gloves cover the face" instruction. Glove height differs between the two manuals (eye level vs shoulder height), so there is no single "true" height.

| 2 | "Pose is exaggerated for readability" | OK, fair | Honest and sufficient. Real guards are a small, tight position held all the time, and the manuals describe no "higher cover" state, so the game's lift from the Idle stance is a visual device. Suggest naming the difference once. | [S1][S3] |

## Recommendations (for the designer)
1. Keep the claim as written. Optionally drop "forearms covering" from any text that implies it is sourced, or label it as the game's picture only.
2. Optional wording for the guard abstraction line: "... instead of a guard that is the same tight, always-held stance (lead hand about eye level, rear hand near chin or collarbone, elbows tucked) [S1][S3] ...". This states what real boxing does.
3. Clamp rule for tuning: glove centres between chin and top of head (AC31) agree with both manuals' picture. No change needed.

GAMEPLAY ABSTRACTION: both gloves raised to eye level in front of the face, with a visibly higher Guard than the Idle stance, instead of a lead hand at about eye level or shoulder height and a rear hand near chin or collarbone held constantly [S1][S3] — the player must read the held Guard state from a side-on camera.
