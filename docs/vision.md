# Vision — Boxing (working title)

> **DRAFT for client approval.** Nothing here is final until the client approves it.
> The Producer keeps this file up to date. Changes go into a PR the client reviews.

## One line
A short-session 3D boxing game where you win by reading your opponent and using sound fundamentals.
Button-mashing doesn't win.

## Pillars
1. **Readable boxing fundamentals.** Jab, cross, guard and slip each have a clear purpose, a cost and a
   counter. A player who watches the opponent and manages stamina beats one who spams punches. Animations
   and timing must make the reason for each outcome visible.
2. **Procedural look.** Every visual and sound comes from code: primitives, generated meshes, shaders,
   procedural textures and synthesized audio. No external assets. The style should look deliberate, like a
   clean, stylized sparring gym, not like placeholder art.
3. **Short sessions.** A fight takes a few minutes. You get from launch to the first punch in seconds, and
   the next round or rematch starts with one key.

## Audience
Players who enjoy skill-based fighting games and are curious about real boxing. You don't need to know
boxing to start, and real boxing knowledge pays off.

## Non-goals (for now)
- Licensed boxers, real venues, career mode, online multiplayer.
- Photorealism. Being readable matters more than looking real.

## Client decisions
- 2026-11-21 — **Camera: side-on**, like a classic fighting game. Both boxers stay in view, which keeps
  distance and timing readable (pillar 1).
- 2026-11-21 — **Default difficulty: forgiving.** New players should get to read the opponent and learn
  the fundamentals before being punished for mistakes.
