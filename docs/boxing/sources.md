# Boxing sources registry

Every factual claim about real boxing in this repo cites an entry here as `[S#]`. Only boxing-consultant
adds entries. Source rule: environment-spec.md §7.

## Allowed tiers
| Tier | What counts | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Official rulebooks: e.g. World Boxing technical and competition rules, Association of Boxing Commissions (ABC) unified rules, national boxing commissions or boards | Check and record the **current edition** |
| **2** | Peer-reviewed research | Must have a DOI or PubMed link |
| **3** | Coaching material from credentialed coaches, or national-federation coach-education programmes | **State the credential** (e.g. "national federation Level 3 coach") |

**Not sources:** forums, unsourced videos, blogs, and AI-generated summaries.

## Citation rules
- Every claim cites `[S#]`, and the entry has a URL or DOI and an access date.
- Summarize in your own words. Never copy rulebook text.
- Anything the game deliberately does differently from real boxing is marked
  **"GAMEPLAY ABSTRACTION:"** with a one-line reason.
- When a newer edition appears, add a new row. Don't edit the old one, so old citations stay valid.
