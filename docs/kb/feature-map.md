the right place to change. Maintained by kb-keeper. Rules: no line numbers; every backticked repo path

| Player moves (fight rules) | Keyboard-controlled player boxer: walk A / D (not while punching or slipping; slower while guarding). Jab J (lead hand) and cross K (rear hand), one throw per fresh press (holding or key repeat never throws a second). The timing, reach and cancels are the same in both stances; the stance only decides which glove moves (see Stance). Guard S is a hold: hands come up over a short raise, drop on release, and punches are ignored while it is held. Slip Q (boxer's left) or E (boxer's right): head moves, no walking, only Active evades. Jab recovery can be cancelled into a cross; slip recovery into a jab or cross; nothing else cancels. No input buffer; same-tick priority is slip left, slip right, jab, cross. Walking stops at the minimum separation from the opponent, which never moves or attacks | `src/Core/PlayerFighter.cs` + `PlayerFighter.Tick` (state, phase, walking, bounds, contact), `PlayerFighter.IsBlocking`, `PlayerFighter.IsEvading`, `PlayerFighter.EvadeSide`, `PlayerFighter.Contact`; `src/Core/MoveSet.cs` + `MoveSet.IsTimedMove` | `game/scenes/Main.tscn` | fight_forward; fight_back; fight_jab; fight_cross; fight_guard; fight_slip_left; fight_slip_right | `tests/Core.Tests/PlayerFighterTests.cs`; `game/scenes/smoke/SmokeFight.cs` + `Smoke.RunFightScript` (scripted fight, AC17 to AC24) |
